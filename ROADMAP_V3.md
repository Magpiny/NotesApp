# 🗺️ Notafo Roadmap: The Journey to v5.0

This document serves as the strategic guide for Notafo's evolution. Our core mission is to surpass Google Keep by combining simplicity with professional power, while maintaining a **Strictly Local-First** philosophy.

---

## 🏛️ Version 2: The Foundation (Strictly Offline)

### 🖼️ v2.5 — "The Media Update"
- **Image Attachments**: Support for multiple local images per note with a fluid gallery view.
- **Voice Notes**: Integrated local audio recording and playback.
- **Canvas Mode**: A basic sketching tool for quick drawings or handwritten diagrams.
- **On-Device OCR**: Use local ML kits to extract text from images (searchable offline).

### 📁 v2.6 — "The Structural Update"
- **Nested Notebooks**: Folders and sub-folders for hierarchical organization.
- **Smart Folders**: Rule-based dynamic grouping (e.g., "Recently Edited Work").
- **Archive 2.0**: Auto-archiving logic and better lifecycle management.

### 📅 v2.7 — "The Context Update"
- **Calendar View**: Visual timeline for all tasks and time-sensitive notes.
- **Sticky Notifications**: Persistent notification tray items for high-priority focus.
- **Advanced Reminders**: Improved recurring logic and snooze features.

### 🧠 v2.8 — "The Knowledge Update"
- **Bi-directional Linking**: Use `[[Note Title]]` to connect ideas.
- **Backlinks**: See which notes reference the current one.
- **Direct Edit Widgets**: Interactive home screen widgets for instant updates.

---

## 🚀 Version 3: The Mastery (Cloud & Intelligence)

### 🔄 v3.1 — "The Portability Phase"
1. **Markdown Folder Export**: Export entire notebooks as organized Markdown files.
2. **PDF Generation**: Transform notes into professional PDF documents.
3. **Private Cloud Sync (WebDAV)**: Support for self-hosted cloud sync (Nextcloud, etc.).
4. **Encrypted Dropbox Sync**: Optional encrypted sync for Dropbox users.

### 🏆 v3.2 — "The Intelligence Phase"
5. **On-Device LLM Setup**: Integrate local AI models (Gemma/Llama) for privacy-first AI.
6. **Smart Summarization**: One-tap AI summaries for long notes.
7. **Action Item Extraction**: AI automatically identifies and creates tasks from note text.
8. **Global Search 2.0**: Semantic search (finding notes by "meaning" via local embeddings).

### 🎨 v3.3 — "The Visual Phase"
9. **Freeform Canvas**: An infinite board for visual brainstorming and note layout.
10. **UI 3.0**: Advanced shared-element transitions and predictive back navigation.

---

## 💎 Version 4: The Ecosystem (Financial & Growth)

### 💰 v4.1 — "The Financial Foundation"
1. **Transaction Engine**: Core logic for local spending and income tracking.
2. **Category Management**: Customizable icons and colors for expense types.
3. **Receipt Scanning**: On-device OCR to extract data from shopping receipts.
4. **Monthly Budgeting**: Set and track limits for specific categories.

### 📈 v4.2 — "The Insights Phase"
5. **Financial Dashboards**: Visual charts (Pie/Line) for spending habits.
6. **Recurrent Expense Alerts**: Reminders for bills and subscriptions.
7. **Savings Goals**: Milestone tracking for specific financial targets.
8. **Debt Tracker**: Manage and visualize loan repayment progress.

### 📑 v4.3 — "The Professional Phase"
9. **Tax-Ready Export**: Export financial data to CSV/Excel formatted for accounting.
10. **Account Reconciliation**: Match local records with bank statements manually.

---

## 🧘 Version 5: The Harmony (Resilience & Wellbeing)

### 🌿 v5.1 — "The Habit Phase"
1. **Habit Engine**: Dedicated system for tracking consistency beyond simple tasks.
2. **Visual Progress Heatmaps**: GitHub-style activity maps for your routines.
3. **Streak Protection**: Smart logic to manage habit "rest days" and recovery.
4. **Gamified Milestones**: Local-only achievement system for long-term consistency.

### 🔋 v5.2 — "The Wellbeing Phase"
5. **Mood & Energy Logger**: Integrated daily journaling for internal state tracking.
6. **Productivity Correlation**: Local AI analysis linking mood/energy to task completion.
7. **Focus Guard**: Local app-blocking during scheduled deep-work sessions.
8. **Digital Declutter**: AI suggestions for archiving old tasks or redundant notes.

### 🛡️ v5.3 — "The Sovereignty Phase"
9. **The Ghost Vault**: Advanced encryption for specific folders using unique user keys.
10. **Local-Network Collaboration**: Serverless P2P note/task sharing over Wi-Fi/Bluetooth.

---

## 🛡️ Core Development Principles
1. **Offline-First**: Every feature must work 100% locally before any online connectivity is considered.
2. **Speed & Fluidity**: Maintain a premium Material 3 feel with 60fps animations.
3. **Data Ownership**: No proprietary formats. Data should be easy to move in and out.
